Saves

A save is a copy of your library kept in GitHub. One is made automatically about once a day, and you can make one yourself before a big change.

Setup help

Keep your library in a private GitHub repository so every device you open stays in step. Steps: (1) use your book-tracker repository, or a separate private one; (2) on GitHub open Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens, choose that one repository, and give it Contents: Read and write; (3) paste the repository and token below on each device.

The token is stored only in this browser. Use a token limited to the one repository. Cover images are saved as separate files in a covers folder of the same repository.

How to useOPERATING MANUAL

Add a book

Press + New Entry. Only the title is required. Pick a status: Wishlist, Bought, Currently Reading or Finished. Rate the book from 0 to 100%, and add notes or quotes if you like.

Update your progress

Press Quick update on a card. Type the page you reached or use −10, −1, +1 and +10, then press Save. You can change the status and notes there too. Reaching the last page marks the book Finished, and moving a Wishlist or Bought book past page 0 sets it to Currently Reading.

Edit or delete

Edit opens the full form for every field. Delete asks you to confirm before it removes the book.

Find books

Search looks through titles, authors, genres, languages and notes. Narrow the list with the status buttons and the genre and language menus, and choose a sort order. Clear filters resets everything.

Add a title with many volumes

In the New Entry form, type the title (for example Naruto) and the edition (for example Manga). Tick This title has several volumes and enter a range such as 1 to 70 to create every volume at once. Volumes with the same title and edition are grouped into one series card with a tile for each volume. Press a tile to update that volume's page, status or notes. Bulk update sets a range of volumes to one status, and Add volumes extends the series.

Use the dashboard

Open the Dashboard tab for your totals, the annual goal gauge, books finished per month, status, genre, language and rating breakdowns, series progress and what you are reading now.

Using it on other devices

Your library syncs to your Claude account. Open this page signed in to Claude on any phone, tablet or computer and the same books, covers and goal appear, and changes made on one device show up on the others. Theme and tab choices stay per device. The first time you open it on a new device, the library already saved to your account is loaded. Export a JSON backup now and then as a safety copy. Reset clears the library on every device, so export first if unsure.

Adding fast and changing the layout

The round + New entry button stays in the corner on every tab, and pressing N on a keyboard opens it too. Above the library, choose Cards (full detail), List (one compact row per book) or Covers (a poster wall). In Covers, each book is a small poster showing only its cover. Hover over one to see its title, and tap it to expand the full entry in place. Press Collapse on the entry, or Collapse all at the top, to return to posters. A series shows as one poster with its volume count. The edit window also has Delete entry. The numbers on the status buttons count the books in each status. Status colours: ochre is Bought, teal is Reading, green is Finished, grey is Wishlist.

Several formats of one title

For a title that comes in more than one format (for example Jujutsu Kaisen as Manga, Light Novel and Databook), file each format with the same title and a different Edition. They are grouped into one title. The main format is shown first, and the other formats appear in an Other formats section under its volumes. In Covers layout the poster shows the number of formats.

Add volumes to an existing entry

On any single entry, tap Add volumes. The entry becomes Volume 1 of a series (if it had no volume number) and the volumes you list are filed next to it, sharing its details and cover.

Prologue and half volumes

Volume numbers can be 0 or have decimals. Volume 0 is named Prologue and 0.5 is named Special prologue automatically, and they sort before Volume 1. When adding a series, use Extra volumes to list them, for example 0, 0.5, or give your own names with 0=Prologue, 0.5=Side story. Clear the range to add only the extras. To rename one volume later, edit it and fill in Volume name.

Several genres and the series cover

In the Genre / Tag field, separate genres with commas, for example Fantasy, Adventure, or tap the genre buttons under the field to add or remove ones you already use. Each genre shows as its own tag, the genre filter matches any of them, and the Dashboard counts each one. For a series, give volumes their own covers with Edit, then open a volume and press Use as series cover to choose which cover shows on the series card. Press it again to go back to automatic.

Hiding entries

Press Hide on a book, or Hide series on a series, to take it out of the library list without deleting it. Hidden entries are still saved, synced and counted on the Dashboard. When some are hidden, a Show hidden button appears beside the filters, and Unhide brings an entry back.

Covers and years

In the form, choose a cover image for a book. It is shrunk and saved to your library, and volumes added together share one cover. Fill in the year released and the years you bought, started and finished it. Changing a status fills the matching year with this year if it is empty, and you can edit any year later. The Dashboard shows releases by decade and your activity by year.

Set your yearly goal

Type a number in the Goal box. The meter counts the books you finished this year. The Currency box changes the prefix on prices.

Switch theme and mode

Two buttons control the look. TTPD is the typewriter manuscript theme and Substation Tower is the industrial control-panel theme. Press the button of the theme you are not using to switch to it. Press the button of the active theme to flip between its light and dark modes: The Parchment and The Anthology for TTPD, Daylight Shift and Blackout for Substation Tower. The app remembers your last choice.

Reading the panel

On the meters, green is early progress, yellow is past 60% and red is past 85%. The stripe on each card shows its status: yellow for reading, green for finished, and grey for bought or wishlist.

Back up your list

Open the Data menu in the header. Export backup saves a JSON file, or shows the text to copy if saving is unavailable. Import file and Paste import load a backup and let you replace your library or merge into it. Reset tracker clears every book on all your devices. Your library is saved to your account, but export a backup now and then as a safety copy.
